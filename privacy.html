Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Beecoming Whole Works (“BWW,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you visit our landing page, interact with us through Instagram or ManyChat, request a free resource, schedule a conversation, or otherwise communicate with us, and how we use and protect that information.

This policy is written for the current Version 1 experience. If our tools or data practices materially change, we will update this policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Your name, Instagram username, profile information, and messages when you interact with our Instagram account or ManyChat conversations.
  • Information you choose to share in comments, Story replies, direct messages, consultation requests, or other communications.
  • Your name, email address, scheduling details, and any information you enter when booking through Calendly.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting support, asking questions, or communicating with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical and usage information that may be collected by our website host, browser, device, or third-party platforms, such as IP address, device type, browser type, pages viewed, date and time, referring page, and link interactions.
  • Information collected through cookies or similar technologies if analytics, security, or performance tools are enabled on the landing page.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, financial account credentials, medical records, health insurance information, or other highly sensitive personal information through Instagram, ManyChat, the landing page, or ordinary email.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your messages and continue the conversation you started.
  • To deliver the free resource or experience you requested.
  • To remember limited conversation context so you do not have to start over each time you interact with us.
  • To route you to the resource or next step that matches what you expressed.
  • To schedule and conduct consultations or other requested conversations.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the landing page and conversation experience.
  • To comply with applicable law, protect rights and safety, prevent misuse, and enforce our terms.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

3. ManyChat, Instagram, Meta, Calendly, and Other Service Providers

We use third-party platforms to provide this experience. These may include Instagram and Meta, ManyChat, Calendly, Vercel or another website host, and other service providers used for file hosting, security, analytics, or business operations.

These providers may collect or process information under their own terms and privacy policies. ManyChat may process information from Instagram interactions and messages on our behalf. Calendly may process information you enter to schedule a meeting. The website host may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the landing page.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party platforms. Please review their policies before using their services.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers that help us operate the landing page, messaging, scheduling, file delivery, analytics, security, and business systems.
  • When you direct us to share information or give us permission.
  • To comply with law, legal process, or lawful government requests.
  • To protect the rights, safety, property, and integrity of BWW, our users, or others.
  • In connection with a business reorganization, merger, transfer, or similar transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

5. Conversation Memory and Retention

We may retain limited information such as how you entered the conversation, the resource you requested or received, your last short response, whether a human conversation occurred, and whether you requested a consultation. This helps future conversations feel connected rather than restarting from the beginning.

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights. Retention periods may also depend on the third-party platforms we use.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The landing page or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for essential operation, security, performance, preferences, or analytics. You may be able to control cookies through your browser or through a consent tool if one is displayed. Disabling cookies may affect certain features.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may contact us at beecomingwholeworks@gmail.com to ask about personal information we control, request correction or deletion, or withdraw from future communications. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and some information may be retained when required or permitted by law.

You may stop automated Instagram conversations by not responding, by asking us to stop, or by using any platform controls available to you. You may also unfollow, block, or manage your Instagram and browser settings directly.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. However, no internet transmission, messaging platform, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use care when deciding what information to share online.

9. Data Breach Response

If we become aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will investigate and take appropriate action, including providing notice when required by applicable law.

10. Children

The BWW landing page, resources, and coaching-related conversations are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this experience. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can review and address it.

11. International Visitors

Our business and service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you access the experience from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data-protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our tools, services, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will show when the current version became effective. Material changes will be communicated when required.
